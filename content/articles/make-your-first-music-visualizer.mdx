The quickest way to a music visualizer is to start from a template: a finished design that already moves with the music. You swap in your song, and Vibely does the rest. This takes about five minutes.

<Steps>

<Step>

Open Vibely. Under **Templates**, tap the one you like.

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/tutorials/make-your-first-music-visualizer/01.png"
  alt="The Projects screen with the templates"
  outline={{ x: 0.04, y: 0.556, w: 0.44, h: 0.117 }}
/>

</Step>

<Step>

The template plays with Vibely's demo song. If you like it, tap **Use Template**. If not, close it with the **X** and try another: nothing is saved until you use one.

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/tutorials/make-your-first-music-visualizer/02.png"
  alt="A template playing in its preview, with Use Template at the bottom"
  outline={{ x: 0.04, y: 0.885, w: 0.92, h: 0.058 }}
/>

</Step>

<Step>

Your project opens in the editor. To use your own song, tap the song row, the first row of the timeline.

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/tutorials/make-your-first-music-visualizer/03.png"
  alt="The editor with the template open"
  outline={{ x: 0.49, y: 0.628, w: 0.51, h: 0.054 }}
/>

</Step>

<Step>

In the Music panel, tap **Replace Music**.

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/tutorials/make-your-first-music-visualizer/04.png"
  alt="The Music panel with Replace Music, Trim and Audio Mix"
  outline={{ x: 0.058, y: 0.651, w: 0.884, h: 0.057 }}
/>

</Step>

<Step>

Pick where your song is:

- **Files**: an audio file on your iPhone or in iCloud Drive.
- **Extract From Video**: the sound of a video in Photos.
- **Elsewhere**: how to bring in a song from another app.

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/tutorials/make-your-first-music-visualizer/05.png"
  alt="The menu with Files, Extract From Video and Elsewhere"
  outline={{ x: 0.19, y: 0.523, w: 0.62, h: 0.167 }}
/>

The template keeps its look and now moves to your song. Close the Music panel with the arrow at its bottom left.

</Step>

<Step>

Tap **Play** to watch it with your song.

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/tutorials/make-your-first-music-visualizer/06.png"
  alt="The video playing in the editor"
  outline={{ x: 0.81, y: 0.914, w: 0.12, h: 0.054 }}
/>

</Step>

<Step>

When you're happy with it, tap the purple button at the top right. Check the **Resolution** and **Frame rate**, then tap **Export**.

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/tutorials/make-your-first-music-visualizer/07.png"
  alt="The export settings: Resolution, Frame rate, Estimated file size and Export"
  outline={{ x: 0.637, y: 0.874, w: 0.304, h: 0.061 }}
/>

<Tip>FHD at 30 FPS suits TikTok, Reels and Shorts, and exports faster than UHD or 60 FPS.</Tip>

</Step>

<Step>

Vibely makes your video. Keep the app open until it's done.

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/tutorials/make-your-first-music-visualizer/08.png"
  alt="Exporting, with the percentage done and a Cancel button"
/>

</Step>

<Step>

Your video is saved in Photos. Tap the caption to copy it, then tap **TikTok**, **Reels**, **Shorts** or **More** to post it.

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/tutorials/make-your-first-music-visualizer/09.png"
  alt="Saved in Photos, with the caption and the buttons to post it"
  outline={{ x: 0.147, y: 0.857, w: 0.707, h: 0.085 }}
/>

</Step>

</Steps>

Your template is a starting point. Change its colours, add your cover art or the song title, or add more layers: the handbook explains every part.
