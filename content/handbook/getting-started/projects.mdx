The Projects screen is the first thing you see when you open Vibely. It holds everything you have made, and the templates to start something new.

## What a project is

A project is one video in the making: a song, an optional background video, and the layers and effects you add on top. Vibely saves a project by itself as you work, so there is no Save button. A project is kept as soon as it has a song.

## The Projects section

Your projects are listed newest first. Each row shows a picture of the project, its name, its shape (such as 9:16 or 16:9) and when you last changed it.

- **Show All / Show Less.** The list shows your five most recent projects. When you have more, tap **Show All** at the end of the list to see every project, and **Show Less** to fold it again.
- **No Projects.** Before you make your first project, the section says so and points you to the templates below.

## What you can do with a project

Tap a project to open its menu:

- **Open** opens it in the editor.
- **Rename** gives it a new name.
- **Duplicate** makes a copy you can change without touching the original.
- **Share as Template** saves the project as a template file you can send to a friend or keep as a starting point. The file keeps the layers and effects. Your song becomes Vibely's demo song, the background video is left out, and your own pictures become the Vibely logo.
- **Delete** removes the project after you confirm. Deleting can't be undone.

You can also swipe a project to the left to delete it.

## The Templates section

Under your projects is the Templates section, the quickest way to start.

- **Blank** opens an empty project. Add a song first, then build it up layer by layer.
- **Import** opens a template file someone shared with you, made with Share as Template.
- **Templates.** Every numbered card is a finished design you can open and make your own. Tap one to open it in the editor with Vibely's demo song, then swap in your own song.

Opening a template makes a new project, so you can open the same template as many times as you like.

## The menu

The **Menu** button at the top right holds:

- **Settings**
- **Leave a Review** and **Recommend Vibely**
- **Get Support**, to write to us
- **Legal**: the Privacy Policy, the Terms and the Credits for the music, photos, fonts and code Vibely uses
