The Projects screen is the first thing you see when you open Vibely. It holds everything you have made, and the templates to start something new.

A project is one video in the making: a song, an optional background video, and the layers and effects you add on top. Vibely saves a project by itself as you work, so there is no Save button. A project is kept as soon as it has a song.

Until you close it, a banner at the top, **New: the Vibely Handbook**, opens this handbook with **Open Handbook**. Its **X** hides it until you next open Vibely.

<MarkerHeading n={1}>New Blank Project</MarkerHeading>

The **+** button opens an empty project. Add a song first, then build it up layer by layer. A blank project is only kept once it has a song.

<MarkerHeading n={2}>Import Template</MarkerHeading>

Opens a template file someone shared with you, made with **Share as Template**. It opens as a new project with Vibely's demo song.

<MarkerHeading n={3}>Menu</MarkerHeading>

The **⋯** button holds:

- **Settings**
- **Education**: the **Handbook** you are reading, and **Tutorials** with step-by-step guides. Both open on this website.
- **Leave a Review** and **Recommend Vibely**
- **Get Support**, to write to us
- **Legal**: the Privacy Policy, the Terms and the Credits for the music, photos, fonts and code Vibely uses

<MarkerHeading n={4}>Projects</MarkerHeading>

Before you make your first project, the section says **No Projects**. After that it lists your projects, newest first. Each row shows a picture of the project, its name, its shape (such as 9:16 or 16:9) and when you last changed it.

Tap a project to open its menu:

- **Open** opens it in the editor.
- **Rename** gives it a new name.
- **Duplicate** makes a copy you can change without touching the original.
- **Share as Template** saves the project as a template file you can send to a friend or keep as a starting point. The file keeps the layers and effects. Your song becomes Vibely's demo song, the background video is left out, and your own pictures become the Vibely logo.
- **Delete** removes the project after you confirm.

You can also swipe a project to the left to delete it.

<Caution>Deleting a project can't be undone, and swiping it away deletes it without asking.</Caution>

The list shows your five most recent projects. When you have more, **Show All** at the end of the list shows every project, and **Show Less** folds it again.

<MarkerHeading n={5}>Templates</MarkerHeading>

The quickest way to start. Every numbered card is a finished design you can make your own.

Tap a template to see it play with Vibely's demo song. Tap the video to pause it. When you like it, tap **Use Template**: Vibely makes a new project from it and opens it in the editor, where you swap in your own song. Close the preview with the **X** to keep browsing; nothing is saved until you use a template.
