Vibely turns a song into a music video. You pick a song, add a background video if you like, and stack **layers** on top: spectrums, waveforms, particles, text, your cover art and more. Most of them move with the music. **Effects** such as glow, blur or VHS change how a layer or the whole video looks. When it looks right, you **export** it as an MP4 video, ready for TikTok, Reels or YouTube Shorts.
